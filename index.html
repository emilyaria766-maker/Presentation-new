📌 Q#1
WHAT IS DECISION MAKING? 🤔
Spoiler: It's not flipping a coin... mostly
📚 OFFICIAL DEFINITION (Textbook Wali)

Decision making is the process of identifying a problem or opportunity and selecting the best course of action from available alternatives to achieve organizational goals. Managers must evaluate options systematically — or at least pretend to.

🧕 Pakistani Translation: "Bhai, kuch karna hai toh pehle sochna parta hai — warna assignment ki tarah last minute mein sab kuch bigad jata hai." 😅
🧠 WHY IT MATTERS
  • ✔ Solves organizational problems
  • ✔ Uses available resources wisely
  • ✔ Achieves business goals
  • ✔ Minimizes risks (hopefully 😂)
📋 KEY ELEMENTS
  • 🎯 Identify the Problem
  • 🔍 Gather Information
  • ⚡ Evaluate Alternatives
  • ✅ Choose Best Option
📌 Q#1 CONTINUED
4 WAYS MANAGERS DECIDE 🧠
Because one method is never enough — just like one biryani plate 🍛
🧠
RATIONAL MODEL
The ideal, textbook approach. Manager identifies problem → sets criteria → weighs options → analyzes alternatives → picks the best one. Very logical. Very structured. Very rarely used in real life.
"Exam mein parha, life mein kabhi nahi kiya" 😂
6-Step Process
⚡
BOUNDED RATIONALITY
By Herbert Simon. Managers make "good enough" decisions with limited info and time. They satisfice — pick the first acceptable option, not the perfect one. "Theek hai, chalta hai!"
"Itna kafi hai, aage bado yaar!" ⚡
Satisficing Approach
💡
INTUITION-BASED
Experienced managers use gut feeling and subconscious knowledge built over years. They just KNOW. "20 saal ka tajurba hai — trust me bro." Studies show 89% of senior managers use this frequently!
"Mujhe pata hai karna kya hai — bas!" 🔥
Experience-Based
🎭
EVIDENCE-BASED
Using real data, research, and analytics to make decisions. Modern and scientific. "Google karo, references dhundo, Excel banao — phir decide karo." The most professional approach when time allows.
"Google kiya? References hain? Baat karo phir!" 📊
Data-Driven
🔍 DEEP DIVE
RATIONAL MODEL: 6 STEPS 🧠
"The Perfect Student" approach — jo actual mein koi nahi karta
1
Identify the Problem
"Yaar, problem kya hai?"
2
Set Decision Criteria
"Kya important hai?"
3
Allocate Weights
"Rate karo 1-10"
4
Develop Alternatives
"Aur options?"
5
Analyze Alternatives
"Sab check karo"

🎯 REMEMBER: RATIONAL IS THE THEORY
Real managers use bounded rationality + intuition + data TOGETHER.
"Combination use karte hain bhai — ek se kaam nahi chalta!" 😅
📌 Q#2
TYPES OF DECISIONS 🎯
Programmed vs Non-Programmed — fancy words, simple idea
📋
PROGRAMMED

Definition: Routine, repetitive decisions handled through standard procedures and rules. Lower management level. Auto-pilot waali decisions.

  • Reordering office supplies monthly
  • Standard employee leave approvals
  • Monthly payroll processing
  • Daily attendance tracking
🟢 "Yeh kaam toh auto-pilot pe hota hai bhai!" 😴
LOWER MANAGEMENT — EASY MODE
🔥
NON-PROGRAMMED

Definition: Unique, unstructured decisions requiring custom solutions. Top management level. Kabhi hua hi nahi tha wala situation.

  • Launching a new product line
  • Acquiring another company
  • Responding to economic crisis
  • Entering a new market
🔴 "Yar yeh toh kabhi hua hi nahi tha — ab kya karein?!" 😰
TOP MANAGEMENT — HARD MODE
🧠 KEY DIFFERENCE — YAD RAKHNA
PROGRAMMED
Routine ✓ — Repeated ✓ — Rules exist ✓ — Lower level ✓
NON-PROGRAMMED
Unique ✓ — Complex ✓ — No rules ✓ — Top level ✓
🌡️ CONDITIONS
DECISION MAKING CONDITIONS 🌡️
3 conditions that describe your life as a manager (and as a student 😭)
✅
CERTAINTY
🟢 EASY MODE — TENSION NAHI
Manager has complete knowledge of the outcome before deciding. All information is known. Zero guesswork. Pure confidence.
"Pata hai kya hoga — no tension, sab plan mein hai!" 😎
Exams mein guaranteed A chahiye? Same feeling.
⚠️
RISK
🟡 NORMAL MODE — PROBABILITY GAME
Manager can estimate the probability of outcomes. Data exists but outcome isn't 100% guaranteed. Like MCQ mein 2 options eliminate ho jayen.
"60% chance hai — karo ya nahi? Ek dum MCQ jaisa lagta hai!" 🎯
❓
UNCERTAINTY
🔴 HARD MODE — ANDHERA HI ANDHERA
Manager cannot predict outcomes. No reliable data. Pure judgment required. The most challenging condition — complete darkness.
"Kuch nahi pata bhai — andhera hi andhera hai. Last minute paper jaisa feel." 😭
